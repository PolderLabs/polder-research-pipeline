# Decisions

Decision records, comparison matrices, and risk assessments. Every significant choice gets documented here with rationale and alternatives considered.

## Accepted Decisions

Closed decisions with recorded rationale, alternatives evaluated, and consequences tracked. Each is a durable note with an ADRC structure.

## Pending Decisions

Open questions awaiting resolution. Each entry captures the context, options on the table, and what evidence or reasoning is needed to close it.

## Decision Records

Individual decision notes using ADRC format: **A**ssumption, **D**ecision, **R**ationale, **C**onsequences. Filed under the relevant domain.
