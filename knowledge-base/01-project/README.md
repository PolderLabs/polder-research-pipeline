# Project

Goals, requirements, ethics, and stable constraints. Notes here define what the project is and cannot be.

## Goals and Vision

What does success look like for the Polder Research Pipeline? What problems does it solve, and for whom?

## Requirements

Functional and non-functional requirements. Each requirement should be testable or verifiable.

## Constraints

Stable constraints: things that cannot change without a decision. Includes budget, timeline, regulatory environment, and technical boundaries.

## Ethics and Safety

Constraints that must never be violated. Safety properties, privacy guarantees, responsible-AI commitments, and review gates.
